Colordoku

One mark per row, column, and color. Marks cannot touch.

Generating puzzle

i
Ready Cross off impossible squares, then place marks.

Input

Drag to cross off. Double-click, double-tap, or right-click to place a mark.

  • ←↑↓→ Move
  • Home End Row start or end
  • Space X Cross off
  • Enter M Place mark
  • ⌘/Ctrl Z Undo
  • ⌘/Ctrl ⇧ Z Redo

Hint

Rules

  • Exactly one mark in every row.
  • Exactly one mark in every column.
  • Exactly one mark in every color.
  • Marks cannot touch, even diagonally.
